The Need

A nation of quiet longing.

Japan is the world’s second-largest unreached people group. Beneath its beauty and order lies a spiritual weight — one that only the Gospel can lift.

Our Mission

Sent by hope. Sustained by grace.

Nevertheless Missions exists to see the Gospel take root across Japan — through church planting, missionary care, and long-term partnership with the Japanese Church.

We aren’t a passing project. We’re a community of families and supporters committed to the slow, faithful work of ministry in a land where every convert is a miracle and every church is a seed.

Our name comes from Peter’s response in Luke 5. He had fished all night and caught nothing. But at Jesus’ word — “nevertheless” — he tried again. We do the same.
